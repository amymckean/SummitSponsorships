Our Program

Who We Are

Summit Fastpitch is the girls' softball program of Summit United, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, serving athletes in the Maple Valley area through our 12U and 14U teams. We focus on intentional coaching, individual development, and competitive opportunities that help young women grow on and off the field. Everyone who coaches and runs Summit is an unpaid volunteer, so your support goes to the athletes.
