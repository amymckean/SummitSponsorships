Who we are

Summit Fastpitch is the only fastpitch-focused program in Maple Valley, built to provide structured, long-term development for female athletes. It is run by Summit United, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Everyone who coaches and runs Summit Fastpitch is an unpaid volunteer, so your support goes to the athletes.
